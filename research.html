Research and evidence

Understanding Water Wastage Across Our Campus.

What we observed, measured and heard on campus: the evidence base for WaterWatch.

How we gathered the evidence

A mixed method approach combining direct observation, measurement and interviews, so findings rest on more than one source.

  1. 1

    Observe usage and wastage

    Walk-throughs of selected campus locations to observe how water is used and where it is lost.

  2. 2

    Baseline measurements over three days or more

    Consistent daily readings at each location using a recorded method and unit.

  3. 3

    Interview Estates/Facilities staff

    Understand how problems are currently reported, prioritised and repaired.

  4. 4

    Interview at least five students

    Capture user experiences, habits and barriers to reporting.

  5. 5

    Document verified problems

    Photograph and log leaking taps, broken pipes, overflowing tanks and other confirmed issues.
