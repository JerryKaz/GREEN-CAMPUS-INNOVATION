About this project

Green Campus Process Innovation is a student project by Group 5 at Valley View University, Ghana, focused on water conservation. VVU WaterWatch is a proposed prototype, not an official university system.

Your privacy

  • We collect only what is needed; names and emails are always optional.
  • Submissions stay in the browser of the device used to submit them; nothing is sent to a server.
  • The project team reviews submissions on the presentation device through the admin panel.
  • Data is used solely for this conservation project and is not shared or sold.
  • To have a submission removed, use the feedback form and quote any reference you were given.

For official university matters, please contact the relevant Valley View University office directly.
